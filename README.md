## English

### 1. App Support
For any questions, feedback, bug reports, or account deletion requests regarding the **Gezdim** app, please contact us:
- **Support Email:** enfusirket@gmail.com
- **Response Time:** Within 24-48 hours.

### 2. Privacy Policy
- **Location Data:** Your device's GPS location is used strictly in real-time to detect your current city and offer map check-in suggestions. Your location data is never saved on our servers or tracked.
- **User Data:** The cities and districts you check in are stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure linked to your user account and are never shared with third parties.

### 3. Terms of Use & PRO Membership (EULA)
- **PRO Membership:** In-app purchase (Lifetime PRO) unlocks premium SVG templates, watermark-free sharing options, and an ad-free experience permanently.
- **Payments:** Purchases are billed through your Apple ID / App Store account according to standard Apple EULA terms.
